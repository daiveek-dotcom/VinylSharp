Privacy Policy

Last updated: 07 November 2025 · Vinyl Sharp Private Limited

This Privacy Policy explains how Vinyl Sharp Private Limited (“Vinyl Sharp”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you use our website and services, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

Information we collect

We collect your name, contact number, email address, shipping address, order history, payment identifiers and similar details needed to process your orders, deliver products, and prevent fraud.

How we use your data

  • Fulfilling and delivering your orders
  • Generating invoices and coordinating delivery
  • Responding to your enquiries
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations
  • Preventing fraud and misuse

Data retention

Personal data related to orders is retained for a minimum of five years as required by applicable law. Payment data is handled in line with RBI guidelines. Account deletion is available once your orders are complete and any return windows have expired.

Your rights

You may withdraw consent, request corrections, access your information, and request deletion. Some requests may be delayed where they relate to active orders, fraud investigations, or legal requirements.

Contact

For any privacy request, contact us at support@vinylsharp.com.
