Our story

Bringing analog sound back to India

Vinyl Sharp is a curated online portal for records — a place to discover the format, order with confidence, and take that warmth home, wherever in India that is.

Why we exist

Records deserve better than a playlist

Streaming made music infinite and forgettable. We started Vinyl Sharp for the people who still want to hold an album, read the sleeve, and sit with a record from start to finish.

So we built a portal Indian collectors were missing: a collection worth digging through, ordered securely and delivered with the same respect the format deserves — no matter which city you're in.

What we stand for

Why choose Vinyl Sharp

Each promise below describes exactly what it says — no borrowed manufacturing language.

01

Curated by real listeners

The portal's shelves are chosen by people who love the format — across rock, jazz, blues, pop and more. Every record earns its place because we'd play it ourselves.

02

A secure, straightforward order flow

Encrypted checkout, clear order tracking, and a real person to talk to if anything's unclear. Buying a record online should feel as easy as it sounds.

03

Handled like the fragile thing it is

Vinyl warps, scratches and scuffs if you're careless. We aren't. Every order is packed in rigid, record-specific mailers so it reaches you flat and ready to play, anywhere in India.
