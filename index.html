Frequently asked questions

What is Vinyl Sharp?

Vinyl Sharp is an online portal for curated vinyl records, shipping across India. Everything is ordered and paid for on the portal.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. All payments are processed through an encrypted, secure checkout. We never see or store your card details directly.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in India. Every order ships with tracking, so you always know where your package is.

How do you make sure records don't get damaged in transit?

Every order ships in rigid, corner-protected mailers built specifically for vinyl, so records arrive flat and unwarped. Fragile handling isn't an add-on — it's standard.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major cards, UPI, and net banking through our secure payment gateway.

What if I need to return or exchange something?

See our Refund Policy for eligibility and the return process. For anything urgent, message us directly.

How can I reach you?

Message us on WhatsApp for the fastest reply, email support@vinylsharp.com, or use the contact form.
